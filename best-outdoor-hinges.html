Buying guide

The best outdoor hinges

Reviewed June 2026 · independent, spec-first guidance

Outdoor hinges face rain, sun, and temperature swings. The right grade for your climate is what keeps them from streaking rust down your door.

  • Match the grade to your climate
  • Our picks by use case

What to look for

01

Grade by climate

304 for inland and dry climates; 316 within a few miles of the coast. Hot-dip galvanized is a budget outdoor option but lacks the finish of stainless.

02

Security pins

For out-swing exterior doors, non-removable-pin (NRP) hinges stop the door being lifted off.

03

Weatherproof finish

A quality satin or coated finish resists UV fade and surface staining.

04

Self-closing for gates

Outdoor gates, especially around pools, often need self-closing hardware.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Buying 304 "because it's stainless" within sight of salt water, it will streak and pit within a season or two.
  • Skipping non-removable-pin (NRP) hinges on an out-swing exterior door, which lets someone tap the pins and lift the door off.
  • Pairing stainless hinges with plain carbon-steel screws, the screws rust and can set up galvanic corrosion. Use matching stainless fasteners.
  • Assuming a bright finish means marine grade. Look for a 316 or A4 stamp, not just shine.
  • Under-counting hinges on heavy solid-core or oversized doors, which overloads each knuckle and causes sag.

Frequently asked questions

Do stainless outdoor hinges need oil?

Not really. Stainless runs dry; a little dry PTFE or silicone lube stops squeak without attracting grit. Skip heavy oils outdoors, they hold dust and grime.

Will 316 ever stain outdoors?

In harsh salt air even 316 can pick up light surface tea-staining, but it won't structurally pit the way 304 does. A wipe-down with fresh water or a mild stainless cleaner restores it.

Is galvanized as good as stainless outdoors?

Hot-dip galvanized resists rust well and costs less, but the coating can eventually wear and it lacks the clean look of stainless. Near salt, stainless 316 wins.

What's an NRP hinge?

Non-removable pin, the pin can't be tapped out from outside, so an out-swing door can't be lifted off its hinges. A basic security upgrade.
